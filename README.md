HAAV WIND-TUNNEL MODEL - 0.5 scale of the 800 mm-span design (span 400 mm, wing chord 55 mm, MS(1)-0313, fuselage 50 mm diameter, about 345 mm long with tail)
Fits a 450 x 450 mm tunnel: span 400 mm = 89 % of the width (30-50 mm lower scale gives more margin; ask), height about 70 mm, frontal blockage about 3 %.
print_plates/: 2 plates for a 220 x 220 mm bed (tallest part about 100 mm). single_parts/: every part as STEP + STL (assembly position). assembly/: all parts as separate solids.
Sweep: SG90 servo -> pinion 14 teeth -> left gear 35 -> right gear (module 1, involute, 0.2 mm backlash). 35 degrees of sweep = about 88 degrees of servo travel. Collision-free to 37 degrees (checked every degree).
Hardware: SG90 servo, 2 pivot rods 2 mm x 13 mm (floor to roof), 4 dowels 1.5 mm x 3 mm, 1 screw M1.6/M2 for the roof standoff, glue.
Walls are 1.0 mm (print with 0.4 mm nozzle, 2-3 perimeters). Plug joints between fuselage shells: glue. Side mounts have a saddle that sits on the straight band of the tail section. Tail is fixed (no hinged surfaces).
Props: 3-blade, hub bore 1.6 mm; fix to the pod rear with a 1.5 mm pin or glue.
